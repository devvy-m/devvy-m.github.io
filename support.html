Support

Email tailbytesupport@gmail.com and a real person will answer. Two people make this game, so please allow a few days.

It helps to include your iPhone model, your iOS version, and what you were doing when something went wrong.

Common questions

I bought something and do not have it

Open Settings in the game and tap RESTORE PURCHASES. That asks Apple for everything you have bought with your Apple Account and gives it back. It works on a new phone too. If it still does not appear, email us with the date of the purchase.

I want a refund

Apple handles all payments and all refunds for App Store purchases. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot issue refunds ourselves, and we never see your payment details.

I lost my progress

Scores, streaks and settings are saved on your phone only — the game has no accounts and no cloud save. Progress moves to a new phone with an iPhone backup, and is lost if you delete the app. Purchases are the exception: RESTORE PURCHASES brings those back at any time.

I want fewer ads

The Supporter Pack in the shop removes the ads between runs. Restarting after a failed run is always free and never shows an ad.

I want to change my privacy or tracking choices

Open Settings in the game:

  • PRIVACY OPTIONS reopens the consent form, if one applies where you live.
  • Tracking is controlled by iOS, not by us: iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking, then turn Pizza Cutter off.

See the privacy policy for what is collected either way.

Something is broken, or I have an idea

Email us. Bugs and suggestions both land in the same inbox and both get read.
