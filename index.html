Pizza Cutter: Cozy Slices

A cozy arcade game about cutting pizza faster than the belt moves.

One swipe cuts the pizza. Keep up with the orders, keep the toppings together, and keep the belt moving. That is the whole rule — the rest is in your hands.

Pizza Cutter is made by two people. It runs entirely on your phone, needs no account, and works offline apart from ads, purchases and these links.
