About the game

Pizza Cutter: Cozy Slices is a pixel-art pizza shop with one rule: swipe to cut. Everything else is in how well you cut.

Slice Rush

Pizzas roll out of the oven and down a belt, and each one needs halving before it reaches the box. Cut straight through the middle for a PERFECT 50/50. The belt speeds up, the pizzas start arriving two at a time, and three strikes end the run. Starting again is always free.

Endless Shift

Not everyone likes the same thing on their pizza. Cut every order so each topping ends up alone on a slice of its own, before the clock runs out. Solve one in a single stroke for a ONE STROKE bonus.

The Daily Special

Ten orders a day, the same for everyone: cut each pizza into the shape of its box, from a circle to a heart to a star. One scored try, a streak to keep, and a result card to share.

The Midas

Every so often a golden pizza turns up. Cut it into as many slices as you can before it’s gone, all the way to 150 for ALL GOLD!

Features

  • One finger, one swipe: easy to learn, hard to master.
  • Short runs, made for a coffee break.
  • Cutters to earn as you play, from a Knight’s Sword to a Laser Cutter.
  • Two dozen toppings and five crusts.
  • Seven optional shop themes that change the look, never the game.
  • Game Center leaderboards. No account or sign-in.

About the developer

Pizza Cutter: Cozy Slices is the first game from Devon Marshall, a software engineer turned solo developer in Florida. The idea came from her husband, who asked one day, “What about a game where you endlessly cut pizzas?” Devon did everything else: the code, the design, the game flow, and every pizza in between. It’s the first step toward a small family studio, built on the ideas the two of them dream up together. When she isn’t making games, you’ll find her cooking, playing games, or out walking with her two dogs.
