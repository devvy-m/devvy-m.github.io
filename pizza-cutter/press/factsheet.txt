TINY (about 150 characters)
A cozy pixel-art pizzeria on iPhone where one swipe cuts the pizza. Three modes, a daily shape
puzzle and cutters to earn. Free on the App Store.

SHORT (about 70 words)
Pizza Cutter: Cozy Slices is a pixel-art pizzeria on iPhone where one swipe cuts the pizza. Halve
pizzas racing down a conveyor belt in Slice Rush, give every topping its own slice in Endless Shift,
and cut a new set of shapes in the Daily Special, the same ten orders for everyone. Short runs,
cutters to earn, and Game Center leaderboards. Free on the App Store, made by a solo developer.

ABOUT THE DEVELOPER
Pizza Cutter: Cozy Slices is the first game from Devon Marshall, a software engineer turned solo
developer in Florida. The idea came from her husband, who asked one day, "What about a game where
you endlessly cut pizzas?" Devon did everything else: the code, the design, the game flow, and every
pizza in between. It's the first step toward a small family studio, built on the ideas the two of
them dream up together. When she isn't making games, you'll find her cooking, playing games,
or out walking with her two dogs.

Everything in this kit is free to use in coverage of the game.
