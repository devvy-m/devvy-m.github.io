Pizza Cutter: Cozy Slices

Privacy policy

Last updated 24 October 2026. Applies to the iOS app Pizza Cutter: Cozy Slices (bundle identifier com.pizzacuttergame.pizzacutter) and to this website.

Pizza Cutter is made by two people. There are no accounts and no servers of ours, and your progress stays on your phone. Three things do reach other companies: advertising data, anonymous gameplay statistics (from version 1.1), and your best scores if you use Game Center (from version 1.1). This page says exactly what and why.

The short version

  • We hold nothing about you. We run no servers and no database of our own, and we cannot identify you.
  • Your progress stays on your phone. Scores, streaks, settings and the items you own are stored on the device.
  • Ads are one exception. Our advertising partners — Unity LevelPlay, Unity Ads, Google AdMob and Liftoff — receive device and advertising information in order to choose and measure the ads you see.
  • Gameplay statistics are another (version 1.1 and later). Unity Analytics records how the game is played, under an anonymous ID, so we can see what to improve. In the EU and the UK this happens only if you allow it.
  • Leaderboards are Apple’s (version 1.1 and later). If you are signed in to Game Center, your best scores are posted to its leaderboards under your Game Center name.
  • Apple handles money. Purchases go through the App Store. We never see your name, card or address.
  • You can change your mind. Tracking is an iOS switch, and PRIVACY OPTIONS in the game’s Settings reopens your consent choices.

What stays on your device

The game saves the following in its own storage on your iPhone. It is not sent to us, and we cannot read it. The gameplay statistics and leaderboard scores described below are the only parts of it that leave the phone:

  • Your scores, best runs and daily streak.
  • Your settings, such as sound, music and which theme is equipped.
  • Which cosmetic items and packs you own.
  • Whether you have seen the tutorial, and your answers to the consent and tracking prompts.

Deleting the app deletes all of it. An iPhone backup carries it to a new phone. Purchases are the one thing that survives deleting the app, because Apple — not we — remembers them.

Advertising

The game shows ads between runs, and offers optional rewarded ads: to continue a run, and to peek at an order in the Daily Special. Ads are served through Unity LevelPlay, which asks Unity Ads, Google AdMob, Liftoff and other ad networks for an ad to show. To do that, those companies receive information from your device, which typically includes:

  • Device and app information — device model, operating system version, language, time zone, and the app’s identifier and version.
  • Identifiers — a vendor-scoped device identifier, and Apple’s advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allowed tracking when iOS asked.
  • Approximate location — derived from your IP address, at country or region level. The game never asks for or uses precise GPS location.
  • Ad interactions — which ads were requested, shown, watched to the end, clicked, or failed to load.
  • Diagnostics — crash reports and performance data from the ad and consent software, used to keep ads working. These are not linked to you.

We use this only to show ads and to see how much the ads earned in total. We do not receive a profile of you, and we cannot look up an individual player.

Gameplay statistics (version 1.1 and later)

From version 1.1, the game uses Unity Analytics to record how it is played, and Unity Remote Config to fetch a few game settings (such as how often ads play between runs and how closely a Daily Special shape has to match). Both are part of Unity Gaming Services. They send Unity:

  • Gameplay events — a run starting and ending (the mode, the score, how long it lasted and how it ended), Daily Special orders and their grades, cutters earned, screens opened, settings such as sound and theme, whether an ad was offered, watched or skipped, your answer to iOS’s tracking prompt, and whether a purchase succeeded (the item and its price, never any payment details).
  • Ad earnings — for each ad shown, the ad network and what it earned, so we can see which ads pay.
  • Identifiers — a random ID for this installation and an anonymous player ID that Unity creates. Neither contains your name, email or Apple Account.
  • Device information — device model, operating system version, language, and a country derived from your IP address.

When: outside the EU and the UK, from when the game starts. In the EU and the UK, only if you allow Unity on the consent form; until then neither Unity Analytics nor Remote Config is started, and the game uses its built-in settings. You can change that answer at any time in Settings › PRIVACY OPTIONS.

Separately, the Unity engine the game is built with sends Unity basic performance and crash information about the device and the app.

We use these statistics to understand how the game is played and to tune it. We do not sell them, and they do not tell us who you are.

Leaderboards (version 1.1 and later)

The leaderboards in Records are Apple Game Center’s. If you are signed in to Game Center, the game submits your scores in Slice Rush, Endless Shift and the Daily Special to Apple, which shows your best ones to other players under your Game Center name. Signing in is optional, and the game never receives your Apple Account details. Apple’s policy, linked above, covers Game Center; you can sign out in iOS Settings › Game Center.

Purchases

The Supporter Pack and the cosmetic theme packs are bought through the App Store using Apple’s in-app purchase system, with Unity’s In-App Purchasing package inside the app. Apple is the merchant: your payment method, billing address and Apple Account are handled by Apple and are never sent to us or stored by us. The app receives only a confirmation from Apple that a purchase succeeded, and records on your device that you own the item.

RESTORE PURCHASES asks Apple what you have bought. That request goes to Apple, not to us.

Consent and tracking choices

If you are in the EU or the UK, a consent form appears shortly after your first run, provided by Google’s User Messaging Platform. Your answer decides whether the ads you see are personalised. You can reopen it at any time: in the game, Settings › PRIVACY OPTIONS. Declining does not stop the game or lock anything — you will simply see non-personalised ads.

If you are in a US state with an applicable privacy law, no form appears by itself. Settings › PRIVACY OPTIONS in the game opens it, with a Do Not Sell or Share choice.

Tracking is separate and belongs to iOS. Before iOS asks, the game explains why it is asking. Whatever you answer, you can change it later in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. If tracking is off, the advertising identifier is not shared and ads are less relevant; nothing else changes.

You can also reset the advertising identifier itself in iOS Settings, which disconnects it from anything previously recorded against it.

Children

Pizza Cutter is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. It is not published in the App Store’s Kids category. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, email us and we will look into it.

How long data is kept

We keep nothing on servers of our own. On-device data lasts until you delete the app. Advertising data and gameplay statistics are retained by our partners under their own retention schedules, which are described in their policies linked above. Leaderboard scores are kept by Apple in Game Center.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port personal information held about you, and to object to or opt out of its sale or sharing for targeted advertising. Because we hold no personal information and cannot identify you, the practical routes are:

  • Opt out of personalised ads — Settings › PRIVACY OPTIONS in the game. In covered US states this form includes a Do Not Sell or Share choice.
  • Stop sharing the advertising identifier — turn tracking off in iOS Settings.
  • Stop gameplay statistics — in the EU and the UK, Settings › PRIVACY OPTIONS in the game. Elsewhere, deleting the app stops them.
  • Leave the leaderboards — sign out of Game Center in iOS Settings.
  • Delete on-device data — delete the app.
  • Reach our partners — Unity, Google and Apple each have their own request process, linked in the table above.

You are also welcome to email us and we will help as far as we are able.

This website

These pages are plain HTML on GitHub Pages. They set no cookies, load nothing from other sites and run no scripts or analytics. GitHub serves them, and like any web host it processes request information such as your IP address; see GitHub’s privacy statement.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts collecting something new, this page is updated before that version reaches the App Store, and the date at the top changes with it. The App Store listing’s privacy labels are kept in step.
